Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Controller: Thomas Menzl Softwareentwicklung

1. Overview

SimpleTD is a tower-defense game for iPhone and Mac (Catalyst). The app includes no third-party tracking, ads, or analytics SDKs.

2. On-device data

Progress and settings are stored locally on your device, including:

  • Campaign progress, high scores, meta credits, and upgrades
  • Audio, language, and display preferences
  • Status of optional content unlocks (in-app purchases)

This data does not leave your device via our servers — we do not operate a game-progress backend.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store / StoreKit). Payment and account data are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive full payment details.

4. No sharing

We do not sell or share personal usage data with advertising networks or data brokers.
